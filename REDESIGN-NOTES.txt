ALPHABET INFOSYSTEMS - REDESIGN NOTES (v4, motion update)

Design
- Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque (headings), Hanken Grotesk (body), Caveat (small handwritten touches only).
- Palette: indigo primary and sky-blue secondary, page background #EAE8FD (primary-100). Tokens are at the top of style.css.
- Mobile menu panel is 260px wide. No horizontal scrolling at any width.
- Dark logo variants for dark backgrounds: assets/logo-light.svg and assets/logo-tagline-light.svg.
- Motion (v4), patterns from motion.dev and MotionSites, plain JavaScript, no library:
  * Hero headline: word by word blur-in, 100ms apart. Hero art springs in, the handwritten arrow draws itself.
  * Section headings: each word rises out of a mask.
  * Service cards, steps, facts: staggered spring entrance. Work card and form note open with a clip reveal.
  * Service cards tilt toward the pointer on desktop.
  * Buttons: label rolls letter by letter on hover and keyboard focus.
  * About text: words light up as you scroll.
  * Process: line fills and step numbers switch on as the line reaches them.
  * Header hides when scrolling down and returns when scrolling up.
  * Hero blobs and the work card grid drift slightly with the scroll.
  * Fade between index and case study page (browsers with view transitions).
  * Riverflow waves: four water layers per wave. They slide with the scroll at different speeds, rise in once, and swell with scroll speed. They stay still when you stop scrolling. For a slow drift while idle, set riverIdleSpeed in script.js (for example 0.02).
  * Reduced-motion is respected everywhere.
- Contact form: Formspree endpoint is configured in script.js as https://formspree.io/f/mvkaowdp.
- SEO: index and case-study pages include page-specific titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, social metadata, robots directives and a sitemap reference.

Before publishing
1. Confirm the Formspree endpoint ID in script.js and its email verification.
2. Set whatsappNumber and geetvaUrl in script.js if you want those links to appear.
3. Upload the whole folder including assets/. If assets/ is missing on the host, the logo falls back to its alt text.
4. Check that canonical and social preview URLs match the final hosting address.
