The brief

Buyers of industrial components compare suppliers on specifications. Geetva needed a website that looks as exact as the parts it makes, and that a buyer can read quickly on a phone or a desktop.

The approach

  • A blueprint and spec-sheet visual language, with clear labels and measured spacing.
  • A brass-toned palette on a clean base, so the products stay the focus.
  • Plain page structure that reads well on small screens.

What was delivered

A complete business website for Geetva Enterprise, designed and developed by me and now running in production.
