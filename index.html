Questions before you write

Short answers to what clients usually ask first.

How do I get a price?

Send me a short message about what you need. I reply with questions if something is unclear, then send a written estimate with the scope and the price. Nothing starts before you agree to it.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the size of the project, so I give the timeline in the written estimate instead of guessing in advance.

Can I see a project you have built?

Yes. Geetva Enterprise, a precision brass and metal manufacturer in Jamnagar, is live in production. Read the case study.

Will my website work on a phone?

Yes. Every website I build is designed for small screens first and checked on a phone before it goes live.

What do you need from me to start?

A short description of your business and what the website or software should do. If you have a logo, photos, product details or examples you like, send them too. If you do not, we can work around it.

I am not in Rajkot. Can we still work together?

Yes. I work by email and calls, so your location does not limit the project.

What happens after the site goes live?

I stay available for updates and fixes. Ask me about ongoing support when you request the estimate.
